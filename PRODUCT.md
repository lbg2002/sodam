# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop listeners on Ubuntu GNOME who want a native, low-latency Soda Music client that remains useful while browsing, searching, and listening in the background.

## Product Purpose

SodaM brings the core Soda Music listening workflow to Linux: discover music, search the catalog, manage playlists, play and cache tracks, read lyrics, and control playback from the desktop.

## Brand Personality

Immersive, expressive, dependable. The interface should feel close to the official Soda Music client while remaining calm and responsive during playback.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic dashboard look, a dense settings-first layout, decorative motion that delays listening, and flat gray pages that make cover art feel secondary.

## Design Principles

1. Let the current cover and song state lead the visual hierarchy.
2. Keep playback controls reachable and stable while pages change.
3. Use motion to explain state changes, loading, and navigation.
4. Keep dense music lists scannable with consistent row behavior.
5. Preserve clear feedback when network, cache, or audio state changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, scalable UI text, visible focus/hover states, and a reduced-motion setting for users who prefer less animation.
